// About

Complex projects, real partners

An afternoon where industry, technology and research meet, for the people leading complex digital projects in the medical device sector. One concrete question at its core: how do you build digital solutions that are truly integrated, scalable and compliant in today's medtech?

01 / Who it's for

CTOs, R&D managers, innovation leads, executives, product owners and project managers working on digitalization and re-engineering in medtech, medical electronics, pharma, biotech and healthtech companies.

02 / What we talk about

AI in clinical diagnostic workflows, risk-driven re-engineering (technical debt, cybersecurity, compliance), and what sets a strategic partner apart from a supplier.

03 / Why

To build concrete connections between companies, research and technology to handle the complexity of developing medical solutions.

Why attend

  • Discuss real problems, not theoretical scenarios
  • Learn how to combine AI, UX, software architecture and regulatory work sustainably
  • Hear the industry's view on partnerships and scalability
  • Make new, qualified professional connections

What does it take to build a medical product today?

The first edition put AI, imaging, architecture, interoperability, research, industry and ecosystems on the table. In 2027 we take a step further: not a single technology, but the journey that turns an idea or a promising technology into a medical product that is usable, verifiable, manufacturable and sustainable over time.

The journey work in progress

Three stages that tell how an innovation becomes a real device. Topics and programme are being defined.

  1. 01
    BUILD IT

    Technology & design

    Architecture, software, hardware/embedded, AI, data, interoperability, UX and Human Factors. Not which technologies are new, but which choices make a product solid from day one.

  2. 02
    MAKE IT REAL

    From technology to product

    Clinical validation, regulatory, cybersecurity, usability, industrialization and quality: the step from prototype to real device.

  3. 03
    MAKE IT LAST

    Growth & sustainability

    Lifecycle, scalability, regulatory and technology change, supply chain, business models, investment, partnerships and market access.

MEDTECH NEXT 2026

Complex projects, real partners

On 11 June 2026, PoliHub in Milan hosted the first edition of MedTech Next: an afternoon for the people leading complex digital projects in medical devices, from AI built into diagnostic workflows to risk-driven re-engineering of devices, with voices from Politecnico di Milano, Confindustria Dispositivi Medici, BION Lombardy Life Sciences Cluster and industry.

Programme

Talks were held in Italian; titles translated.

  1. 15:00From vision to collaboration: the strategic context of MedTech Next 2026Gabriele Dubini — Politecnico di Milano
  2. 15:20From the machine to AI: (really) building an integrated diagnostic workflowSimone Manini — D/Vision Lab

    A project born from three companies with complementary skills: imaging, artificial intelligence and medical devices. The goal: bring AI diagnostics into the real clinical workflow, overcoming technology silos, integration challenges and usability constraints. The result? A concrete, scalable solution that is already in use.

  3. 15:40Audience Q&A
  4. 15:50From local innovation to the global market: lessons from the medtech industryAlessandro Rotilio — Medical Device Industry Executive
  5. 16:10Beyond technology: what the data say about MedTech in ItalyValeria Glorioso — Confindustria Dispositivi Medici Research Centre
  6. 16:30When AI becomes a system and transforms development, integration and clinical workflowPasquale Pedicini — GMM Group
  7. 16:45AI and innovation models in medtech: vision and strategy for growthSofia Mosci — Camelot Biomedical Systems
  8. 17:10Risk-driven re-engineering for medical devicesMarco Ciambrone — NRG

    In MedTech the challenge is no longer just designing new products, but keeping existing ones alive amid constant regulatory, technological and cybersecurity change. A risk-driven software re-engineering approach born from hands-on work with manufacturers and industrial partners: architectural analysis, technical debt management and controlled integration of new modules, turning complexity into a competitive advantage.
